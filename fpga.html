FPGA Spectrum Analyzer

An RTL design in VHDL that samples microphone input, performs real-time frequency analysis, and renders a spectrum analyzer and heatmap over VGA.

Overview

Real-time audio processing requires strict deterministic timing and high-throughput parallel computation capabilities. FPGAs are highly suited for Digital Signal Processing (DSP) operations like the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) due to their inherent parallelism and ability to process streaming data without instruction-fetch latency. The objective of this project is to create a fully integrated Audio Spectrum Analyzer that responds instantaneously to ambient audio and visualizes frequency content seamlessly.

The project emphasizes hardware architecture design, state-machine-driven data flow, I2C/I2S communication protocols, and complex mathematical realization in VHDL without the reliance on a soft-core processor. By managing all peripheral interfacing and DSP algorithms through dedicated custom logic, the system minimizes latency and maximizes throughput.
